## Feedback 1 — what the BRGSA paper actually asked

The paper was materially more direct than Dungeon had modelled. Most work was recall followed by a
bounded application of a named framework. The difficult part was remembering the correct method and
applying it cleanly, not discovering a complicated interpretation hidden in the case.

Owner-reported ten-mark shapes, lightly normalised for spelling:

1. A B2B company has a ₹10,000 budget and five weeks to fundraise. Choose lean-validation methods
   and tests with explicit pass/fail outcomes that would help it reach the goal. Explain the thought
   process and justify each choice.
2. Using AARRR — with the framework's full name supplied — choose a B2B or B2C company, identify one
   part of the framework, and explain how changing it creates a positive or negative ripple effect
   in at least two other parts.

Owner-reported five-mark Section B shapes:

1. A company's blended CAC has increased while the cohort has stayed the same. Investigate the cause.
2. Given a plan's gross margin and CAC, calculate its payback period and suggest ways to shorten the
   period without changing CAC.

The working conclusion is that answers should get to the named framework, calculation or diagnostic
move quickly, use the supplied case facts, and justify the causal link. This is a report of BRGSA,
not an official marking scheme or a prediction of exact future questions.

## Feedback 2 — how Dungeon should teach from that evidence

Study should remain rich enough to teach, but it should not force a learner through the full depth
before revealing the usable exam move.

- Keep a mix of concise exam-shaped application and deeper explanation.
- Lead worked examples with **Case → Answer** so revision reaches the move quickly.
- Reuse the existing in-depth causal explanation under **Why this answer works** rather than deleting
  it or duplicating it in another exam panel.
- Relocate maps, recap material and numerical methods to the lecture where they become relevant;
  do not make a dense map the first thing a learner must parse.
- Author caselets as bounded tasks: diagnose one metric change, perform one supplied calculation,
  select one framework, or recommend against stated constraints.
- Let the module chamber sample direct recall and application. Use evidence from misses to suggest a
  narrow repair; do not require every concept to be read and tested as a pair.
- Treat the complete Study notes as the primary learning surface and the former Learn machinery as
  targeted repair for demonstrated weak concepts.

Two BRGSA worked examples now act as the concrete authoring references:

- `BRGSA-M03-L03`: rising blended CAC with unchanged cohorts → investigate channel mix, traffic cost,
  funnel conversion and sales effort before blaming customer quality.
- `BRGSA-M07-L05`: calculate monthly gross profit and payback → recommend ARPU, gross-margin or cash-
  collection levers while holding CAC constant.
