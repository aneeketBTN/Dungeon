# Tester announcement draft — Examiner timing and the released IBM case

Dungeon's Examiner now gives you three clear choices:

- **Full mocks · 1+ week out** — the real two-hour paper shapes, with feedback held until
  submission so you have time to diagnose and repair the result.
- **Speedrun · within a week** — eight coached questions, one from every module, with teaching
  after each answer. Rotate them across your final week.
- **Minis · last 25–30 minutes** — a last-pass retrieval routine: map the eight modules, answer
  eight prompts without notes, then protect the easy-to-lose marks.

IBM now also has a **Released case** paper. It uses the exact prompt we received, makes the missing
assumptions explicit, and applies ten course lenses to one coherent inclusive-business model. Use
that paper for the actual brief; keep using the numbered IBM sets to practise transferring the
frameworks rather than memorising one model.

On Learn, the four full subject cards now fold into a smaller subject switcher after you choose one,
so it stays obvious where you are without occupying the whole page.

One access change: Dungeon still asks you to keep your account personal and use one browser at a
time, but it will no longer automatically lock you out for opening another device or changing
country/network. If anything looks wrong, message Aneeket privately rather than sharing access.

Please report the subject, screen, device width, and what you expected when something feels unclear.
