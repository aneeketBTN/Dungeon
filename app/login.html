Tester group membership

Closed testing runs through the private WhatsApp tester group. Join it, stay in it, and provide good-faith feedback for the duration of the test: updates, feedback requests, and questions all happen there. A tester who does not join and does not participate after reminders will be removed from the cohort. Treat everything posted in that group as private test material under the confidentiality terms below. If you leave the group or ask to stop testing, tell Aneeket so your access can be closed cleanly.

Personal use and confidentiality

You may use Dungeon personally, give feedback, discuss it with authorised testers in the private group, and recommend it in broad terms. Outside that group, do not share literal or substantially similar questions, options, answers, explanations, passages, caselets, prompts, question-bank structure, screenshots, recordings, photographs, copied text, dashboard or progress views, detailed feature behaviour, private workflows, scoring, difficulty or recommendation logic, unreleased designs, access links, security details, or private feedback.

Personal access only

Your approved email acts as your Dungeon password. Do not give it to another person for access, let another person use your logged-in browser, or let anyone else practise in your account. Use one active browser at a time and sign out before changing devices.

No copying

Do not scrape, bulk-download, reverse engineer, plagiarise, republish, sell, adapt, or use private material to reproduce Dungeon or create a substantially similar question bank, dashboard, workflow, or product. Ordinary use and good-faith bug reports are allowed.

Security and data

Dungeon stores your approved email, revision progress and attempts, an opaque session-token hash, timestamps, first-login Cloudflare country code, agreement version, and account-lock state. It does not request precise GPS. Cloudflare may process ordinary network and security metadata. One active browser is permitted. A country change can lock the account for Aneeket to review; travel, VPNs, mobile networks, and routing can cause legitimate changes.

Your written answers

When you write an answer in written practice, Dungeon sends it to an AI model to be checked against the course material. That model may be Cloudflare's, or a third-party provider reached through a routing service that can change if one is unavailable, so the company handling a given answer is not knowable in advance and may be outside India. Nothing identifying you is sent with it: the model receives the question, your writing, and the course passages, and never your name, email, or account.

Dungeon keeps the text you wrote for up to three months. This is not needed to run your own session, so it is worth being clear about: the machine marking is not yet reliable enough to be treated as a grade, and it is improved by comparing what it decided against what a person reading the same answer would decide. Your own results are shown to you; the stored text is used to correct the rubrics and write better prompts for the students who take this course after you. It is not published, sold, or shown to other testers.

You can ask Aneeket to delete your answers at any time, sooner than three months, without giving a reason, and withdrawing from the test deletes them with everything else.

If something you write reads as personal distress rather than an exam answer, Dungeon stops before checking: that response is not sent anywhere, not marked, and not stored. The full privacy notice explains all of this.

Revocable access

Access is a revocable invitation. A confirmed material breach may result in immediate permanent removal from Dungeon, future closed tests, and other Aneeket-controlled services, subject to applicable law. Revocation deletes active Dungeon sessions and server-side Dungeon progress. Dungeon is an early revision aid, may change or contain errors, and does not guarantee an exam result.
