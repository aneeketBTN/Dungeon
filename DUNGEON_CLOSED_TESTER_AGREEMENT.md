# Dungeon Closed Tester Participation Agreement

**Effective date:** 11 August 2026  
**Product owner:** Aneeket Das  
**How you accept:** tick the acknowledgement box at your first Dungeon login  
**Your access:** the approved email you gave Aneeket  

## Plain-language commitment

Dungeon is a small, private exam-season test. You are being trusted with unfinished product ideas,
learning content, and access that is not available to the public. You may recommend Dungeon in
general terms. You may not reveal, copy, or help another person access the private test.

## 1. What you may do

- Use Dungeon personally for revision and provide honest feedback.
- Discuss the test with Aneeket and authorised testers inside the designated private community.
- Tell someone, in broad terms, that you found the revision experience useful or recommend the
  product, without describing its private content, screens, mechanics, or implementation.
- Ask Aneeket to invite a friend separately if you think they should try it.

## 2. What must stay inside the test

Do not share outside the authorised tester group:

- literal or substantially similar questions, answer choices, answers, explanations, passages,
  caselets, prompts, or question-bank structure;
- screenshots, screen recordings, photographs, copied text, or exports from the website;
- dashboard or progress views, detailed feature behaviour, private workflows, difficulty logic,
  scoring or recommendation mechanisms, unreleased designs, or access links; or
- private feedback, test instructions, security details, or other unpublished material supplied by
  Aneeket.

This does not prevent a vague recommendation or a high-level statement that Dungeon is a revision
tool. When unsure, ask Aneeket before sharing.

## 3. Personal access only

Your approved email acts as your Dungeon password. Do not give that email to another person for
the purpose of entering Dungeon, let another person use your logged-in browser, or allow another
person to practise in your account. Use one active browser at a time and sign out before changing
devices. A friend who wants access must be invited by Aneeket under their own email.

## 4. No copying or competing reproduction

Do not scrape, bulk-download, reverse engineer, plagiarise, republish, sell, adapt, or use private
test material to reproduce Dungeon or build a substantially similar question bank, dashboard,
workflow, or product. Do not help another person do so. Ordinary use and good-faith bug reporting
are allowed.

## 5. Security and data notice

To provide access, save progress, and identify obvious account sharing, Dungeon stores your
approved email, revision progress and attempts, an opaque session-token hash, timestamps, your
first-login Cloudflare country code, and account-lock state. It does not request precise GPS
location. Cloudflare may process ordinary network and security metadata.

Only one active browser session is permitted for an approved email. A request from a different
country than the first login can immediately lock the Dungeon account. Travel, VPNs, mobile
networks, and routing can create legitimate country changes, so a lock is reviewed by Aneeket and
is not by itself proof of misconduct.

## 6. Access and consequences

Access is a revocable invitation, not a purchase or permanent entitlement. Aneeket may suspend
access while investigating a security signal. A confirmed material breach - including credential
sharing, giving unauthorised access, publishing private materials, or attempting to copy the
product - may result in immediate permanent removal from Dungeon, future closed tests, and other
Aneeket-controlled services, subject to applicable law. Revocation deletes the tester's active
Dungeon sessions and server-side Dungeon progress.

## 7. Early-test limits

Dungeon is provided for testing and revision support. It may change, contain errors, or become
temporarily unavailable. It does not guarantee an exam result and does not replace official course
materials, instructions, or academic judgement.

## 8. Acceptance

No signature is needed. The first time you sign in with your approved email, Dungeon shows a short
version of these terms, and you tick **"I have read and agree to the closed tester terms"** and
choose **Accept and enter**. Dungeon records only which version you accepted and when.

That tick confirms you understand the boundaries above, will keep your access personal, and will
ask Aneeket before sharing anything that may reveal the private test. If you would rather not
agree, close the page and tell Aneeket; nothing else happens.

Questions, correction requests, account-lock reviews, withdrawal, or deletion requests should be
sent privately through the contact channel used for onboarding.
